**SendMyBag:** Mostly positive for European routes. Main complaints are about non-EU customs fees (not applicable Spain→Netherlands). "Cheaper than alternatives, reliable delivery 95%+ on time."

**MyBaggage:** Mixed. Some say great, others report: "DON'T USE — worst customer service I've ever experienced." A Reddit thread titled "mybaggage.com took down my negative review on Trustpilot" is a 🚩.

**Eurosender:** "Scam — don't use. Booked express, took 7 days. No refund." "Surprise charges at delivery." High volume of negative reviews despite 4.0 score.

**BagExpress:** Smaller but positive — good reviews for Italy-Spain-Europe luggage routes.

**Packlink:** "Horror stories" — acts as broker, when things go wrong you're stuck between Packlink and the actual courier.

## Steps to Book

1. Measure suitcase dimensions (L×W×H in cm, including wheels) and weigh it
2. Go to chosen website → enter origin (Galicia) → destination (Netherlands)
3. Input 23kg weight + dimensions (standard suitcase ~75×50×30cm)
4. Choose standard/economy (3-5 days) or express (24-48h)
5. Pick collection date (door pickup from your address)
6. Pay online → print label → attach to suitcase
7. Leave suitcase at door on collection day
8. Track online until delivered

## Important Notes

- **EU internal shipping** — no customs forms needed (Spain → Netherlands)
- **Prohibited items:** aerosols, loose lithium batteries, flammable liquids
- **Insurance:** Free cover up to €150-200 usually; add extra for valuables
- **Standard suitcase dimensions** (~158cm total L+W+H) are fine at 23kg
